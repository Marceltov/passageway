# Licence

**Free for private use. Companies need a licence.**

| | Licence | Who may use it |
|---|---|---|
| The core, the bundled connectors and embedders | [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0) | Individuals for their own use, and noncommercial organisations such as charities, schools and research institutions. A company, a freelancer working for a client, or an employee at work needs a commercial licence. |
| The SDK, the protocol pages and their JSON Schemas | MIT | Anyone, for anything: write a connector or an embedder of your own without asking. |
| The sample corpus | CC BY-SA 4.0 | Anyone, with attribution and the same licence. |

A licence changes who may use passageway, not what it does. There is no hosted service, no feature held back for paying customers and no licence check in the software ([V8](values.md)); passageway never reports anything home ([V2](values.md)).

Releases up to and including 0.2.9 were published under AGPL-3.0-or-later and stay so for everyone who has them.

## Asking for a licence

Open an issue titled "Licence" on [GitHub](https://github.com/Marceltov/passageway/issues) without company details, and the maintainer will reply with a way to talk.
