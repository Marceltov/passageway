A licence changes who may use passageway, not what it does. There is no hosted service, no feature held back for paying customers and no licence check in the software ([V8](values.md)); passageway never reports anything home ([V2](values.md)).

Releases up to and including 0.2.9 were published under AGPL-3.0-or-later and stay so for everyone who has them.

## Trying it at work

Install it and try it for up to 31 days under the Free Trial licence ([`LICENSE-FREE-TRIAL`](https://github.com/Marceltov/passageway/blob/main/LICENSE-FREE-TRIAL)). No conversation needed. Keeping it after that needs a licence.

## Commercial licences

**Not on sale yet.** A licence will be a fixed yearly price for a company, bought on a page without negotiation, and it buys the legal right to use passageway at work. It holds nothing back: the software is the same, with no licence check in it. Support is best effort through the issue tracker or one email address, with no promised response time. Setup help is a paid session on request; there is no consulting.

## Review

The licence is reviewed once, twelve months after the first release under it, and loosened if it holds back adoption; it is not tightened.

## Asking for a licence

Open an issue titled "Licence" on [GitHub](https://github.com/Marceltov/passageway/issues) without company details, and the maintainer will reply with a way to talk.
