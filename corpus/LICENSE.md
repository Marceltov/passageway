# Licence of the corpus

The articles in `en/` and `de/` are text from Wikipedia, licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International** licence (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

This differs from the rest of this repository, whose code is AGPL-3.0-or-later. The corpus folder is not covered by the AGPL, and anyone who reuses these articles must keep CC BY-SA 4.0 and give credit.

**Attribution.** Every article file names its source page and revision in its front matter, and `en/ATTRIBUTION.txt` and `de/ATTRIBUTION.txt` list each article with a link to its page history, which names the authors.

**Changes made.** The text was fetched with Wikipedia's plain-text extract and converted to markdown. Tables, images, infoboxes and other formatting are not included; formulas are kept only as TeX between `$` signs (a few appear as loose symbols); headings became `##` headings; and boilerplate sections (such as References, External links, Einzelnachweise and Weblinks) were removed. The wording of the remaining text was not changed.
