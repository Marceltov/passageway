# Contributing

Every change starts from an issue; see `docs/development.md` for the gates and `docs/values.md` for what a change is judged against.

passageway is licensed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 and sold to companies under a separate licence, so the maintainer must be able to license every line. Until a contributor licence agreement is in place, **pull requests from outside are not merged**; open an issue to discuss the change, or write a connector or embedder of your own: the protocols and the SDK are MIT, and a connector in your own repository needs no agreement.
